# Poha — Food Dataset Research Notes

## Food identification
- Food name: Poha
- Regional name: Kanda Poha
- Category: Breakfast / Snack

## Common ingredients
Flattened rice, onion, oil, mustard seeds, curry leaves and spices.
Ingredients and oil quantity vary by recipe.

## Nutrition source
- Source: Anuvaad Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB)
- Record: https://www.anuvaad.org.in/nutrition-fact/poha/
- Reference basis: Per 100 g option selected on the source page.

## Values observed per 100 g
- Energy: 294.53 kcal
- Energy: 1226.35 kJ
- Carbohydrates: 35.05 g
- Protein: 6.09 g
- Fat: 14.14 g
- Fibre: 3.72 g

## Verification notes
- The listed kcal and kJ values do not match the standard conversion exactly.
- Confirm the recipe and serving basis before treating these values as final.
- Do not assume every homemade Poha recipe has the same nutrition values.
