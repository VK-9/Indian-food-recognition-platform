# Poha — Food Dataset Research Notes

## Food identification
- Food name: Poha
- Regional name: Kanda Poha
- Category: Breakfast / Snack

## Common ingredients
Flattened rice, onion, oil, mustard seeds, curry leaves and spices.
Ingredients and oil quantity vary by recipe.

## Nutrition source
- Source: Anuvaad Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB)
- Record: https://www.anuvaad.org.in/nutrition-fact/poha/
- Reference basis: Per 100 g option selected on the source page.

## Values observed per 100 g
- Energy: 294.53 kcal
- Energy: 1226.35 kJ
- Carbohydrates: 35.05 g
- Protein: 6.09 g
- Fat: 14.14 g
- Fibre: 3.72 g

## Verification notes
- The listed kcal and kJ values do not match the standard conversion exactly.
- Confirm the recipe and serving basis before treating these values as final.
- Do not assume every homemade Poha recipe has the same nutrition values.

- The source provides both per-100-g and per-serving values. This record uses the per-100-g reference values; the bowl serving weight has not been confirmed.


# Idli — Food Dataset Research Notes

## Food identification
- Food name: Idli
- Regional name: Idli
- Category: Breakfast

## Common ingredients
Rice, urad dal (black gram), water and salt.

## Preparation notes
The batter is fermented and steamed.

## Nutrition source
- Source: Anuvaad Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB)
- Record: Idli
- Reference basis: Per 100 g option selected on the source page.

## Values observed per 100 g
- Energy: 137.54 kcal
- Energy: 575.46 kJ
- Carbohydrates: 28.18 g
- Protein: 4.64 g
- Fat: 0.33 g
- Fibre: 2.31 g

## Verification notes
- Values are recorded for plain Idli.
- Suji Idli is a separate preparation and is not included in this record.
- Nutritional values may vary depending on ingredients and preparation methods.


# Roti / Chapati — Food Dataset Research Notes

## Food identification
- Food name: Roti / Chapati
- Regional names: Roti; Chapati
- Category: Bread / Flatbread

## Common ingredients
Whole wheat flour, water and salt.

## Preparation notes
The dough is rolled into a flat shape and cooked on a flat griddle. Preparation methods and optional oil or ghee may vary.

## Nutrition source
- Source: Anuvaad Indian Nutrient Databank (INDB)
- Record: Roti / Chapati
- Reference basis: Per 100 g.

## Values observed per 100 g
- Energy: 202.31 kcal
- Energy: 844.75 kJ
- Carbohydrates: 35.65 g
- Protein: 5.88 g
- Fat: 3.56 g
- Fibre: 6.31 g

## Verification notes
- Values are recorded for Roti / Chapati.
- Ingredients and preparation methods may affect nutritional values.
- The kcal and kJ values show a small conversion difference and should be retained as listed by the source until verified.
